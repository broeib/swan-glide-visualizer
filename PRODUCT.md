# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Music producers, neo-soul instrumentalists, jazz keyboardists, guitarists, and short-form social music video creators. They want to visualize, understand, and share lush harmonic progressions with smooth voice-leading that captures the serene aesthetic of @ultraviolet_chorus.

## Product Purpose

A web-based neo-soul chord and voice-leading visualizer inspired by Swan Glide and @ultraviolet_chorus. It displays real-time animated cubic Bezier spline ribbons gliding between extended jazz chords, accompanied by synchronized audio synthesis, interactive fretboard/keyboard voicings, and vertical video rendering.

## Brand Personality

- **Warm, serene, tactile**: Acoustic warmth, physical instrument feel, muted rose pastels and deep plums.
- **Editorial restraint**: Clear typographic hierarchy, ample whitespace, intentional minimalism.
- **Hypnotic flow**: Smooth Viterbi voice-leading ribbons gliding without abrupt jumps or visual clutter.

## Anti-references

- **AI SaaS dashboard slop**: Dark mode purple-to-pink gradient buttons, glowing cards, "PRO STUDIO" eyebrow badges, faux iPhone bezels, and noisy crypto/tech widgets.
- **Overcrowded UI overlays**: Fake Instagram interaction buttons, excessive decorative borders, and noisy controls obscuring the music diagrams.
- **Generic typography**: Unrefined default sans-serifs, misaligned superscripts, and harsh high-saturation neon colors.

## Design Principles

1. **The Visualizer is the Star**: The 9:16 harmonic spline canvas and instrument voicings are the core experience. Never obscure them with floating toolbars or dashboard sidecars.
2. **Quiet Luxury over Loud AI Clutter**: Let serene colors, subtle contrast, and generous whitespace signal quality. Avoid flashy gradient fills and gratuitous icons.
3. **Harmonic Fidelity**: Authentic chord spellings, accurate musical accidentals (♭, ♯), and genuine instrument voicings with realistic fret markers and key proportions.
4. **Physical Instrument Tangibility**: Piano keys with realistic fallboard felt and keybed depth; guitar fretboard with authentic wire gauges and pearloid inlays.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast compliance (≥4.5:1 for text against canvas background).
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for ribbon animations.
- Touch targets ≥44x44px for playback and instrument toggles.
- Proper keyboard navigation for transport and harmony generation.
