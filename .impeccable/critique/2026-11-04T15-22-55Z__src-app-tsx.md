## Criterion Breakdown

### 1. Typography & Hierarchy: 10 / 10
- Root letter rendered in 7xl/8xl bold with natural baseline alignment.
- Accurate musical accidental glyph (`♭` / `♯`) placed beside the root letter.
- Step progress indicators cleanly nested at top.
- Redundant handle pills removed to give the chord name unencumbered breathing room.

### 2. Color & Tone: 9 / 10
- Zero generic AI neon gradients.
- Cohesive palette: pastel blush (`#FAF0F4`) with deep plum typography (`#240A34`).
- Rich, distinct voice colors across octaves C2 to C6.

### 3. Layout & Density: 9 / 10
- Pure 9:16 vertical canvas positioned centrally with generous negative space.
- Floating glass dock positioned at the bottom, responsive on desktop and mobile.
- Clean instrument voicings (guitar fretboard with pearloid dots; piano keybed with active voice light pools).

### 4. Motion & Feel: 10 / 10
- Smooth Hermite spline ribbons gliding between chord steps.
- Active playhead intersection nodes with legible note badges.
- Keyboard shortcuts for rapid creative iteration.
